Electro-Hydraulic CNC Press Brake

Our flagship bending machine combines servo-hydraulic drive technology with a modern CNC controller. Dual-cylinder electro-hydraulic synchronization guarantees accurate ram positioning on both ends, delivering consistent angles even on long or off-center workpieces. Ideal for precision fabrication, elevator parts, cabinets, HVAC and structural components.

  • Servo electro-hydraulic sync system — high repeatability (±0.01 mm ram positioning)
  • CNC controller with graphical programming and bend simulation
  • Motorized back gauge with multiple axes (X, R and optional Z, V)
  • Hydraulic crowning / deflection compensation for uniform angles
  • Welded steel frame with stress-relief annealing for long-term rigidity
  • Optional: front support arms, safety light curtains, quick-release tooling

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What tonnage and bending length options are available?

Standard models range from 40 to 400 tons with bending lengths from 1,500 mm to 6,000 mm. Non-standard tonnage and longer beds can be customized (OEM/ODM).

Which CNC controllers can I choose?

We offer DELEM (DA-52S, DA-53T, DA-58T, DA-66T), ESA and Estun controllers. DELEM is the most popular choice for export markets.

How precise is the electro-hydraulic CNC press brake?

Ram positioning repeatability reaches ±0.01 mm, and bending angle accuracy is typically within ±0.5°, thanks to dual-cylinder servo-hydraulic synchronization and hydraulic crowning.
