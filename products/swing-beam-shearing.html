Swing Beam Shearing Machine

The swing-beam (pendulum) design lets the upper blade arc through the cut, reducing friction and blade wear while keeping high cutting speed. With a fixed rake angle optimized for thin to medium sheets, it delivers clean, burr-minimized edges cycle after cycle — a dependable workhorse for workshops producing ducts, cabinets, panels and general sheet parts.

  • Pendulum swing structure: less blade wear and longer blade life
  • High cutting speed for improved daily output
  • Hydraulic hold-downs secure material without marking
  • Motorized back gauge with NC positioning for repeatable cut lengths
  • Blade gap adjustment matched to your material thickness
  • Simple structure, easy maintenance, low operating cost

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a swing beam and a guillotine shearing machine?

A swing beam (pendulum) shear cuts with a pivoting upper beam: faster cycles and longer blade life, ideal for medium and thin sheets. A guillotine shear cuts straight down with an adjustable rake angle, better for thick plates and heavy-duty work.

What materials can the swing beam shearing machine cut?

Carbon steel, stainless steel, aluminum, copper and other metal sheets within the rated thickness. Tell us your material and thickness and we will recommend the right model.

What cutting thickness range is available?

Standard swing beam models typically cover 3 to 12 mm mild steel depending on model. Custom configurations are available for other requirements.
