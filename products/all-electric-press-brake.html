All-Electric Press Brake

The next generation of bending technology. Driven entirely by servo motors and ball screws — no hydraulic oil, no pumps, no heat. The result: up to 40% lower energy costs every year, a quieter workshop, and precision that stays stable regardless of oil temperature. Perfect for electronics enclosures, kitchen equipment, medical devices and high-mix precision fabrication.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much energy does an all-electric press brake save?

Compared with conventional hydraulic press brakes, our all-electric servo models save up to 40% energy. The servo motor only draws power during actual bending movements.

Does the all-electric press brake need hydraulic oil?

No. There is no hydraulic system, so no oil purchasing, no oil changes and no risk of oil leakage — cleaner workshops and lower running costs.

What tonnage range is available for all-electric press brakes?

Standard all-electric models cover roughly 35 to 220 tons. For heavier tonnage requirements we recommend our electro-hydraulic series.
