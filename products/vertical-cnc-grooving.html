Vertical CNC Grooving Machine

Before bending thick plates, V-grooving removes bending stress and guarantees a sharp, precise fold line. Our vertical CNC grooving machine clamps the workpiece against a vertical table while high-speed cutters machine accurate V-grooves — essential for elevator interiors, stainless steel facades, luxury decoration panels and any product where bend radius must be minimized and angle accuracy is critical.

  • CNC-controlled groove depth and position for consistent quality
  • Vertical clamping: excellent for panels requiring high surface protection
  • Sharp bend corners with minimal radius after folding
  • Eliminates bending springback and stress on thick plates
  • Suitable for stainless steel, aluminum, copper and mild steel
  • Multi-blade cutter head for different groove angles and widths
