About ZTGAOKE

A Trusted Sheet Metal Machinery Manufacturer Since 2006

Founded in 2006 as Ma'anshan Zhengtai Hi-Tech Machinery Blade & Die Factory, we first earned the market's trust through high-quality press brake tooling. In 2014 we grew into a full machinery manufacturer, delivering a complete range of sheet metal processing equipment.

Today our machines serve customers across North America, Europe, Russia, the Middle East and Asia, backed by solid craftsmanship, continuous innovation and dependable after-sales service.

  • Full range: press brakes, shearing machines, grooving machines
  • 21 invention patents · CE certified · ISO 9001 quality system
  • OEM / ODM and non-standard customization available
  • Voltage customizable: 220V/380V/440V, 50Hz/60Hz for all regions
  • Export-ready with overseas installation & training support
