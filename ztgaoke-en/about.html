Our Story

From Precision Tooling to Complete Machinery

Ma'anshan Chint Hi-Tech Machinery Manufacturing Co., Ltd. was established in 2006, originally as the Ma'anshan Chint Hi-Tech Machinery Blade & Die Factory. Our early success came from high-quality press brake tooling, which earned the trust of customers and gave us deep insight into what makes bending truly precise.

In 2014, as the business grew, the company was officially renamed and entered a more comprehensive, standardized stage of modern manufacturing — developing and producing a full range of sheet metal processing equipment.

Today, our main products include the full series of CNC press brakes, shearing machines, grooving machines, presses and non-standard customized equipment. With solid craftsmanship and a spirit of continuous innovation, our machines are sold across China and exported to overseas markets, earning customer trust through reliable performance and complete after-sales service.
