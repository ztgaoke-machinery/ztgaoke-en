About ZTGAOKE

A Trusted Sheet Metal Machinery Manufacturer Since 2006

Founded in 2006 as Ma'anshan Chint Hi-Tech Machinery Blade & Die Factory, we first earned the market's trust through high-quality press brake tooling. In 2014 we grew into a full machinery manufacturer, delivering a complete range of sheet metal processing equipment.

Today our machines serve customers across China and overseas markets, backed by solid craftsmanship, continuous innovation and dependable after-sales service.

  • Full range: press brakes, shearing machines, grooving machines
  • 21 invention patents and 4 industry certifications
  • OEM / ODM and non-standard customization available
  • Export-ready with overseas installation & training support
