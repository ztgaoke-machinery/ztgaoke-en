Bending Machines

All-Electric Press Brake

The next generation of bending technology. Driven entirely by servo motors and ball screws — no hydraulic oil, no pumps, no heat. The result: up to 40% lower energy costs every year, a quieter workshop, and precision that stays stable regardless of oil temperature. Perfect for electronics enclosures, kitchen equipment, medical devices and high-mix precision fabrication.

  • Up to 40% energy saving — power is consumed only during actual bending
  • Oil-free design: clean production, no oil disposal or leak risk
  • Faster approach and return speeds for higher throughput
  • Dual servo-motor sync with closed-loop position feedback
  • Low noise (<75 dB) and minimal maintenance requirements
  • CNC controller with offline programming and USB program transfer
