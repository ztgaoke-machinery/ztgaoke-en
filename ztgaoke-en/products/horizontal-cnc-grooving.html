Horizontal CNC Grooving Machine

The horizontal layout makes loading and aligning large panels fast and effortless — simply place the sheet flat on the table, clamp it, and let the CNC carriage machine precise V-grooves. Compared with vertical models, it excels in batch processing and heavy plates, making it a favorite for door manufacturers, panel processors and high-volume grooving operations.

  • Flat horizontal table: easy loading of large and heavy panels
  • High-throughput CNC grooving with automatic positioning
  • Multiple clamps along the table to secure workpieces firmly
  • Adjustable groove depth, width and angle via tooling options
  • Strong frame design suited to continuous industrial use
  • Custom table lengths available to match your product range
