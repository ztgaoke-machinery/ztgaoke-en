Guillotine Shearing Machine

Built for heavy-duty straight cutting, our hydraulic guillotine shear moves the upper blade vertically along rigid guides for maximum cutting force and squareness. The adjustable rake angle lets you optimize between cutting force and plate distortion — from thin stainless to thick structural plates. The right choice for steel service centers, shipbuilding, pressure vessel and heavy fabrication industries.

  • Adjustable rake angle to match material thickness and cut quality
  • High-rigidity welded frame, annealed and precision machined
  • CNC back gauge with cutting counter and program storage
  • Blade gap quick adjustment (hydraulic or manual options)
  • Square-arm and front support with T-slots and ball rollers
  • Optional: sheet support system, lighting line, extended back gauge
